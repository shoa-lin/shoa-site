This time I ended up at Wangzai Roast Chicken (旺仔烧鸡) in Tangqiangang, Hengsha Village, out in Guangzhou’s Jinshazhou. At night the red sign is visible from a long way off, and the doorway stays lively. What they’re known for here is the signature roast chicken; the black-pepper pork knuckle and the pickled-greens stir-fried intestine are the other crowd favorites.

The whole roast chicken (招牌烧鸡) comes out glossy-skinned; the black-pepper knuckle (黑椒圆蹄) lands still sizzling on an iron plate; the intestine dish (酸菜炒大肠) gets onion slivers and a little bird’s-eye chili. About ¥60 a person — honestly great value.
